Interactive Physics

High-Energy Physics Simulations

Numerical simulations and interactive phase-space models covering QCD confinement, relativistic hydrodynamic expansion, heavy-quark transport, and multi-scale hadronic structures.

From Wooden Cube to Quarks

A continuous logarithmic zoom across 17 orders of magnitude[cite: 3]. Transitions from macroscopic cellulose ($10^{-1}\text{ m}$)[cite: 3] down to atomic orbitals ($10^{-10}\text{ m}$)[cite: 3], the $^{12}\text{C}$ nucleus ($10^{-15}\text{ m}$)[cite: 3], and confined quarks with gluon flux tubes[cite: 3].

Color Confinement

Visualizing the linear confinement potential $V(r) = \kappa r$[cite: 2]. Illustrates non-perturbative Schwinger quantum tunneling[cite: 2], color flux tube breakage[cite: 2], and the transition into colorless asymptotic mesons ($\pi^+, \pi^-$)[cite: 2].

Heavy-Ion Collision & QGP

Spacetime dynamics of ultra-relativistic $\text{Pb}\text{--}\text{Pb}$ collisions at $\sqrt{s_{\mathrm{NN}}} = 5.02\text{ TeV}$[cite: 5]. Features Lorentz contraction[cite: 5], initial Glauber overlap geometry[cite: 5], pressure-driven elliptic flow ($v_2$)[cite: 5], and Cooper-Frye hypersurface hadronization[cite: 5].

Spacetime Evolution of HIC

Chronological progression through early Glasma flux tubes ($t=0$)[cite: 4], pre-equilibrium thermalization[cite: 4], hydrodynamic Bjorken cooling[cite: 4], chemical/kinetic freeze-out[cite: 4], and final-state free streaming[cite: 4].

Charm Quark Brownian Motion

Langevin stochastic transport of a charm quark inside a dense quark-gluon bath[cite: 1]. Demonstrates collisional energy loss, momentum dissipation $|p(t)|$[cite: 1], directional drag[cite: 1], and relaxation toward the thermal Jüttner equilibrium[cite: 1].
